## Brand & Style
The design system balances the clinical speed and density of high-tier project management software with the tactile elegance of bespoke stationery. Designed for modern event producers, creative directors, and high-end hospitality teams, the interface removes administrative friction while honoring the ceremonial nature of event design. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Editorial Productivity**—a fusion of modern minimalism, structured data architectures, and warm bespoke materiality. The experience rejects cold, sterile grays and overly casual playful SaaS motifs. Instead, it relies on warm ivory canvases, dense information hierarchies, deliberate typographic scale, and rich tactile accents that suggest luxury paper stocks, metallic foiling, and architectural precision.

## Colors
The palette evokes an artisanal studio environment: linen paper, dark evergreen botanicals, burnished copper hardware, and aged stone.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Canvas / Default Background: `#F7F5F0` (Warm Ivory) creates an organic, paper-like foundation.
  - Surface Primary (Cards, Sheets, Popovers): `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) provides crisp contrast against the canvas.
  - Surface Secondary (Data Grouping, Sub-panels): `#DFE4DC` (Muted Sage Grey) anchors technical sections without harsh visual breaks.
- **Brand Accents:**
  - Primary Brand Accent: `#28594F` (Deep Forest Green) is reserved for primary actions, structural focus indicators, active navigation states, and high-value milestones.
  - Secondary Accent: `#B98265` (Muted Copper) adds warmth to curated moments, VIP badges, featured tiers, and secondary interactive calls.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:**
  - Text Primary: `#202A29` (Deep Charcoal) maintains high legibility with softer contrast than absolute black.
  - Text Secondary: `#5A6664` for supportive metadata and secondary labels.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#E6E4DC` for low-contrast card borders; `#D5D3CB` for structural panel dividing lines and active field boundaries.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success / Confirmed: `#2D7A5D` (Muted Emerald Green)
  - Warning / Pending Review: `#C88228` (Muted Amber)
  - Critical / Overdue: `#B53D35` (Muted Crimson)

## Typography
Typographic pairings express the duality between celebration and productivity.

- **Display Hierarchy:** Headings serving emotional, ceremonial, or marketing contexts utilize an editorial serif (Newsreader). It brings an intentional literary poise to hero sections, invitation previews, event titles, and high-level milestones.
- **Operational & Interface Hierarchy:** Manrope drives all interactive mechanics, data density panels, metrics, navigation bars, tables, and settings. It provides geometric precision with human warmth, retaining structural readability at sizes as small as 11px (`label-caps`).
- **Data & Tables:** Numbers in budget matrices, runs-of-show, and guest counts should always enable tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve vertical alignment across dense rows.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an uncompromising 8-point spatial rhythm tailored for operational density.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12-column system, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and outer margins of `2rem` (32px). Maximum container width is capped at 1440px for content dashboards to prevent line-length degradation.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and margins. Sidebars automatically collapse into bottom drawers or slide-over navigation rails.
- **Rhythm & Structure:**
  - Component internals use `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) consistently to ensure predictable tap targets and scanning speeds.
  - High-density toolbars (run-of-show timelines, seating charts) employ compact vertical increments (`0.375rem` / 6px) to maximize screen real estate.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system avoids loud, un-tinted drop shadows in favor of flat layering paired with warm ambient diffusion.

- **Layered Surfaces:** The base canvas (`#F7F5F0`) sits at Level 0. Work surfaces and functional cards (`#FFFFFF`) rest at Level 1, defined by a 1px solid border of `#E6E4DC` and an extremely faint, tinted contact shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(32, 42, 41, 0.04)`.
- **Flyouts & Menus (Level 2):** Dropdown selectors, date pickers, and tooltips elevate using `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(32, 42, 41, 0.08)` surrounded by a crisp `#D5D3CB` border.
- **Modals & Drawers (Level 3):** Critical dialogs and sheet overlays float above an ivory-charcoal translucent scrim (`rgba(32, 42, 41, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur). Elevated surfaces use `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(32, 42, 41, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
Shapes employ a controlled radius scale reflecting industrial polish softened by stationery craft.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard interactive elements such as text fields, small buttons, dropdown triggers, and data table badges.
- **Large Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Applied to content cards, budget summary containers, itinerary blocks, and modal windows.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Strictly reserved for category tags, status pills, filter chips, and avatar containers.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Background `#28594F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Hover state transitions to `#1E433B`. 8px radius.
  - *Secondary:* Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#202A29`, border 1px solid `#D5D3CB`. Hover state shifts background to `#F7F5F0`.
  - *Accent (Celebratory/VIP):* Background `#B98265`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#A66F53`.
  - Height is standardized at 36px for dense SaaS actions and 44px for primary event creation milestones.

- **Input Fields & Selectors:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E6E4DC`, 8px radius. 
  - Focus state swaps border to `#28594F` with a 1px matching inner ring shadow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #28594F`).
  - Text uses `body-md` in `#202A29` with placeholder text in `#7E8A88`.

- **Cards & Containers:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, 12px radius, enclosed by a 1px solid `#E6E4DC` border.
  - Header actions use subtle 1.5px stroke icons (Lucide-style) in `#5A6664` that shift to `#202A29` on interaction.

- **Chips & Status Badges:**
  - Always rendered in `label-caps` with pill geometry (`9999px` radius) and internal padding of `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
  - *Active / Success:* Background `rgba(45, 122, 93, 0.12)`, text `#2D7A5D`.
  - *Pending / Warning:* Background `rgba(200, 130, 40, 0.12)`, text `#C88228`.
  - *Draft / Neutral:* Background `#DFE4DC`, text `#202A29`.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Size 16x16px with 4px border-radius for checkboxes, circle for radios.
  - Unchecked: 1.5px solid `#D5D3CB` on white background. Checked: `#28594F` fill with crisp white icon checkmark.

- **Domain-Specific Components:**
  - *Run-of-Show Timeline Item:* Left-aligned vertical tracking border (2px `#DFE4DC`) anchored by numbered node markers; expands on row hover to reveal vendor assignments and cue triggers.
  - *Guest RSVP Matrix:* Dense data rows with fixed header, displaying guest tags, meal choices, and table allocations with zebra rows transitioning from `#FFFFFF` to `#F7F5F0`.
